## Logo


Use this logo for the README, launch materials, GitHub social previews, and simple app identity surfaces.

## Direction

The selected logo is a simple rounded-square app icon with a dark background and a cyan `K` mark. The mark hints at radar/project scanning and safe cleanup without needing a wordmark.

Primary colors:

- Dark background: `#101418`
- Cyan mark: `#38BDF8`
- Mint safety accent: `#86EFAC`
- Warm scan dot: `#FDE68A`
- Muted radar strokes: `#94A3B8`

## Usage

- Prefer the icon alone for small surfaces, favicons, GitHub avatars, and social cards.
- Keep enough padding around the rounded square.
- Do not place the icon on another dark rounded square.
- Do not use the later folder-heavy refinement unless the brand direction changes.

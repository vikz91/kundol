## Logo

The sole logo asset is the 512×512 RGBA PNG [`assets/logo.png`](../assets/logo.png). The README displays it at that size.

Description: a bold cyan uppercase `K` on a deep charcoal rounded square. The straight left stem evokes a terminal cursor; the two diagonal strokes meet around a mint centre point to suggest the moment a scan becomes a reviewed action. The large silhouette reads clearly at avatar size.

Use the new icon for the README, GitHub avatar, launch materials, and simple app identity surfaces.

## Direction

The mark is deliberately spare: one letter, one centre point, and generous space. It reflects the current CLI's scan, plan, and optimise flow without implying a dashboard or project registry in the public app.

Intended primary colors:

- Charcoal: `#101820`
- Cyan: `#36C5F0`
- Mint: `#8AF0C5`

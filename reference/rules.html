Rule catalogue ​

Generated from the bundled JSON registry. Status describes catalogue maturity, not a guarantee that a rule can execute on your machine.

Published rules still need supported handlers and live checks. --allow-beta admits only exact code-approved beta bindings. Protected entries are inventory only. See safety and the registry guide.
